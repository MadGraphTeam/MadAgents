# 05 — The maker cannot check itself

> Asked to grade work it produced, an agent confidently praises it. Verification needs a separate, independent agent.

An agent that wrote the work does not see the result — it sees the chain of self-persuasion
that led there, so it praises obviously-mediocre output. You cannot fix this with "please be
stricter"; you manufacture an outsider with *structure* — a separate agent, different
instructions, carrying none of the self-persuasion. Maker-checker: every "I'm done" clears a
check that isn't its own. And the checker must have no stake in the verdict — when referees are
also players, the check is theater.

## In a harness like this

This is the deep *why* behind the reviewer roster: physics / math / numerics are separate cards,
not the consultant re-checking itself, and the reviewer is structurally an outsider that reads the
consultant's subtree and checks it against source. A consultant self-certifying would void the
check — the oracle-capture case is the vivid argument for why. When auditing a harness, flag any
place where the agent that produced an artifact is also the one that signs off on it; verification
must come from an independent agent with different instructions and no stake in the answer.

## Where the books say it

- **Loop** — §05/§04. Generator is not evaluator (the chain of self-persuasion); building a skeptic is far more tractable than making a generator critical of its own work; done is declared by another (the banking maker-checker rule); diversity beats the echo chamber. The deepest treatment in the sweep.
- **Harness** — §08/§17. The evaluator must be separate, adversarial and rubric-graded — engineering a rigorous independent evaluator is far easier than teaching a generator to self-critique (GAN intuition).
- **Src-Analysis** — ch9. Fresh eyes verify another's work; a fresh agent when a first approach was wrong.
- **CC-Guide** — §08. Writer/reviewer beats solo — the author locks into their own thinking; an independent reviewer catches it from another angle.
- **Polymarket** — §08. The UMA oracle capture: once referees are also players, decentralized truth discovery is an empty phrase — verification by the party with a stake is theater.
